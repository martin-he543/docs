## [West Coast, Lana del Rey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oD1diYvfvx4)

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/5Y6nVaayzitvsD5F7nr3DV)

I can see my baby swinging... The timeless words that introduce the wispiest vocals known to humanity. The slowed and reverbed version is even more divine in its sound. Between the introduction to the chorus, and the echoes of the background "oooooohh", there could not be a more perfect song to provide your soundtrack with your lover.

## [I Know the End, Phoebe Bridgers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WJ9-xN6dCW4)

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/0pJW1Xw3aY4Eh6k5iuBkfI)

Reaching an absolutely euphoric climax, this song perhaps best summarises Phoebe Bridgers' album, Punisher, as it arrives at its dazzling conclusion. "This is the end," proclaims Phoebe Bridgers, whilst blaring synths and crashing drums roar over the scream of her voice.

## [Amoeba, Clairo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VR8ooa3G_5M)

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/0HAqq2GcQKyi3s87GuN7jU)

Time for the casual track of the Winter. Amoeba is a low-key track sung over subdued synths, mesmerising the listener in Clairo's vocals. Indeed, the entirety of this song is perfection from start to finish, as the drums begin to pick up.

## [Lavender Haze, Taylor Swift](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mkR_Qwix4Ho)

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/5jQI2r1RdgtuT8S3iG8zFC)

"Meet me at midnight". With those four words begin the blockbuster album of 2022. But its not just the popularity of this record which owe to its success. Besides the seductive saxophone flourishes, slick beat, the opener to Midnights is poised for perfection.

## [Evermore, Taylor Swift ft. Bon Iver](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EXLgZZE072g)

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/3O5osWf1rSoKMwe6E9ZaXP)

The perfect conclusion to 2020's much-forgotten album evermore, Bon Iver and Taylor Swift provide a wintery duet, as the counterpart to folklore's exile. Another piece of sheer pop perfection, this duet, with lyrics such as "and when I was shipwrecked..." are the metaphorical imagery we have come to expect from Taylor Swift.

## [20꞉17, Ólafur Arnalds, Nils Frahm](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iwS9YmF22Po)

[Listen on Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/track/7vbCfkJf89i4s745KbELgr)

A beautiful piece of ambient piano by this ever-dynamic piano duo results in one of the sweetest pieces of piano music produced to date.
