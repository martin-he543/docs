## Introduction

Having transited through mandatory schooling in a manner becoming of a piece of undigested food passing through the digestive system, sucked bone dry of all personality and creativity, finally churned out of the anus, I have been granted with the recent retrospection over which to assess the ups and downs: the successes and the shortcomings.

This release from the metaphorical anus of graduation is the culmination of 14 years of my life; arguably, the most crucial set of years of one's life, which have been dedicated to the pursuit of "education" in the crudest sense.

So then. Was it worth it?
