## How this works

Nothing changed from the main blog's mechanism, just the source folder:

1. Files live in `memrise/posts/` instead of `posts/`.
2. `memrise/posts/index.json` is regenerated automatically whenever one of those files changes.
3. `memrise/index.html` and `memrise/post.html` read from that folder to list and render posts.
